Antigravity CLI (`agy`) is a new agent runtime, so a Google AI Pro, Ultra or free personal plan can back an agent again now that Gemini CLI no longer serves those accounts. Antigravity agents show live status, get their instructions at session start, keep their conversation across restarts, use agy's own permission modes, and never spend paid AI credits. Messages sent to them wait in Incoming messages, as for Gemini.
